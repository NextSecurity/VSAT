# Getting help

VSAT is a community-maintained open-source project, offered with no warranty or SLA (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

## Before asking

1. Run `.\vsat.ps1 -Doctor` and read its output.
2. Check the [FAQ](docs/faq.md), [usage guide](docs/usage.md), [limitations](docs/limitations.md) and [least privileges](docs/privileges.md).
3. Search [existing issues](https://github.com/NextSecurity/VSAT/issues?q=is%3Aissue).

## Where to ask

| Need | Where |
|---|---|
| Question or idea | [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/NextSecurity/VSAT/discussions), if enabled, or a *Feature request* issue |
| Something is broken | [Bug report](https://github.com/NextSecurity/VSAT/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) |
| Missing or wrong check | [Rule request](https://github.com/NextSecurity/VSAT/issues/new?template=rule_request.yml) |
| Security vulnerability | **Privately**, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) |

## Protect your data

Never post real hostnames, IP addresses, credentials, thumbprints, reports or evidence packages in public issues. Use `-Redact`, review the redacted copy yourself, and attach only what is needed.

## What we can't help with

- General VMware, vSphere or NSX administration or licensing
- Interpreting compliance obligations for your organization. VSAT results are decision support, not an audit opinion.
